GameSpy Revival 0.6 - how to play
=================================

A fan-made app for playing old multiplayer games together: groups with text and voice
channels (it works like Discord), friends and DMs, live server lists, and Metal Fatigue
online play. Not affiliated with GameSpy, Discord or the games' owners.
Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Windows only for now.


1. GETTING IN (everyone)
   - Run GameSpyRevival.exe. Nothing to install.
   - Click the invite link your host sent you (gamespy://join/...), or press + on the far
     left, choose "Join a Group" and paste the link or code. Pick a name and press
     Create account. No password needed: this PC is your key.
   - The left rail works like Discord: GS (top) is Home (friends and DMs), then your
     groups, then +, then the controller button for Games.
   - Drag the top bar to move the window, double-click it to maximize.
     The arrows top-left go back and forward (Alt+Left / Alt+Right).

2. CHAT
   - Text channels keep their history. @name pings someone, @everyone pings the group.
   - Hover a message for quick reactions, reply, edit and delete. Right-click a message
     for the full menu. Press Up in an empty message box to edit your last message.
   - The smiley at the right of the message box adds emoji.
   - Click someone's name for their profile card. Right-click them to message, add a note,
     add or remove them as a friend, or ignore them (hides their messages).
   - Click a category name (TEXT CHANNELS, VOICE CHANNELS) to fold it.
     Right-click a channel to mute it.

3. YOUR STATUS
   - Click your name at the bottom left: Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb (no pop-ups or
     sounds), Invisible, or a custom status. You go Idle by yourself after 10 minutes away.

4. VOICE
   - Click a voice channel to join. Hang up, mute, deafen and settings are bottom left.
   - Settings (gear) -> Voice & Audio: mic and speakers, Mic Test, Voice Activity or
     Push to Talk (works while a game has focus), sensitivity, noise reduction, sounds.
   - Right-click someone in your channel to change their volume or mute them for you.
   - Use headphones: there's no echo cancellation yet.

5. GAMES (controller button on the left)
   - Quake III Arena, OpenArena, Unreal Tournament: live public servers. Double-click one
     to join. Use Find or Browse once so the app knows where the game is.
   - Metal Fatigue: the Setup tab installs the online mod into your game folder. If your
     host runs the group, the app points Metal Fatigue at their server automatically.
     Press Play, open Multiplayer, host or join. Leave the game's Search IP box blank.
   - "Invite crew" posts a Join card in your group's #looking-for-game channel.
   - Battlefield 2, Red Alert 2 and Community Projects: links to the communities who keep
     those games online.

6. HOSTING (one person per group)
   - Press + -> Create My Own (or pick a template), name your group, press Create.
     This also runs the Metal Fatigue server. The last page gives you an invite link.
   - Allow it through Windows Firewall when asked.
   - Forward TCP ports 27901 (group) and 27900 (Metal Fatigue) on your router to this PC.
   - Settings -> Host this group -> Copy invite link, and send it to friends.
   - The group server runs in the background: it keeps going after you close the window,
     and can start with Windows. Chat history is saved on the host's PC.

Your settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\GameSpyRevival.
If the app ever crashes, it writes crash.log next to GameSpyRevival.exe. Please send it.
